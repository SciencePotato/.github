# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Discuss the work rather than attacking the person. Welcome questions, assume good intent, and give others room to correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate these expectations. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub rather than opening a public issue.
